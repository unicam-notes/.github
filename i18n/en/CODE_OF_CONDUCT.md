# Code of conduct

[Italiano](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · English

UNICAM Notes is a collaborative space for students, tutors a like.

- Respect people and different experience levels. Communicate politely and provide constructive feedback.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and spam are not allowed.
- Respect privacy: do not share personal data or private communications without consent.
- Respect copyright and intellectual property. Only publish materials you have the right or permission to distribute.
- Always credit original authors when known and identify sources. Do not remove signatures, credits or provenance information.
